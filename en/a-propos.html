Our story

Nalouo Hair was born from a mother-and-daughter duo. Our house rests on more than thirty years of experience in the hair extension industry, passed down from one generation to the next.

More than a brand, Nalouo is a celebration of individuality and confidence.

Our expertise

Our raw Indian hair comes directly from the temples; our Vietnamese hair is chosen for its fineness. By working with local communities, we guarantee natural, luxurious products and respectful sourcing.

Unprocessed, the hair keeps its original colour and texture, and can be coloured up to 613 blonde. Our wigs are made by hand, to measure.

Ethical sourcing

Hair from Indian temples, in partnership with local communities.

Quality control

Every bundle is checked to stay soft, glossy and tangle-free.

Personal advice

The hair consultation is free, at the showroom and by appointment.
