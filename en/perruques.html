Made to measure

Wigs made by hand with 2 or 3 bundles and an HD lace15 to 25 working days · Prices and stock in store

In four choices

We make each wig from your answers. You can also design it with us at the showroom.

1

The texture

Vietnamese straight for movement, Indian wavy or curly for volume. Delivered in its natural colour, or jet black at no extra cost.

2

The lace

A closure covers the top of the head for a natural parting. A frontal runs from ear to ear and allows every style. All are HD film lace, invisible on every skin tone.

3

The length

From 12″ to 28″. From 18″, we use 3 bundles instead of 2 to keep generous volume down to the ends.

4

The cap

Three sizes for a comfortable fit all day. Because wigs are made to measure, a wrong size cannot be exchanged or refunded: if in doubt, we measure your head at the showroom.
