The House

Founded by a mother and daughterOver 30 years of hair expertise
“Thirty years of craft, passed from a mother to her daughter.”

A family story

Nalouo Hair was born from a mother-and-daughter duo. Our house rests on more than thirty years of experience in the hair extension industry, passed down from one generation to the next.

More than a brand, Nalouo is a celebration of individuality and confidence.

Raw hair, chosen at the source

Our raw Indian hair extensions come directly from the temples. By working with local communities, we guarantee natural, luxurious products and respectful sourcing.

Unprocessed, the hair keeps its original colour and texture, and can be coloured up to 613 blonde.

Ethical sourcing

Hair from Indian temples, in partnership with local communities.

Quality control

Every bundle is checked to stay soft, glossy and tangle-free.

Satin bags

Your extensions travel in a reusable satin bag, to keep for storing them.
