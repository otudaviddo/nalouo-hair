FAQ

Your questions

Is the hair really raw?

Yes. Our bundles are 100% raw, unprocessed hair: Indian temple hair and Vietnamese hair that keep their original colour and texture.

Can it be coloured?

Yes, just like natural hair: it can be coloured up to 613 blonde. We advise you at the showroom.

How long does a made-to-measure wig take?

Each wig is handmade in 15 to 25 working days, depending on the texture, lace and length you choose.

How do I order?

There is no online payment: orders and payment are made at the Jette showroom, by appointment. Shipping in Belgium: + €10.
